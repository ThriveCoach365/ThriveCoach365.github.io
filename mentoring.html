What mentoring is, and what it isn’t.

One-to-one mentoring is life-experience guidance, not clinical care.

What Thrive offers

  • Non-judgmental listening
  • Guidance on goal-setting
  • Support in navigating personal challenges
  • Tools for building resilience
  • Help with career and life transitions
  • Faith-rooted encouragement, and more besides

What Thrive does not offer

  • Clinical mental health therapy
  • Medical or psychological diagnosis
  • Crisis counselling

Need deeper support? We provide referrals to qualified, licensed professionals in Kenya and work alongside them where appropriate. If someone is in immediate danger, please contact local emergency services right away.

Note: I am a life experience mentor, not a licensed counsellor or psychologist. I do not offer clinical mental health therapy or medical diagnoses. If psychological or clinical mental health support is required, I work alongside or refer to licensed professionals.

Good to know before you book.

The full details are in the agreement. Here is the short version.

Sessions

Standard sessions run 50 to 60 minutes. Fees are set out in the Fees section above and in the agreement. Payment is by M-Pesa Till Number.

Cancelling or rescheduling

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice. Late cancellations or missed sessions without notice may be charged at the standard rate.

Confidentiality

What is shared stays between mentor and client, except where there is a risk of harm to self or others, abuse or serious neglect, or a legal requirement. For clients under 18, parents or guardians are contacted promptly when safety concerns arise. Parent progress updates (Sponsor tier) share goals, attendance and themes only.

Between sessions

Short scheduling messages by WhatsApp or call are welcome between 8:00 AM and 6:00 PM. Mentoring and crisis support are not offered by text or outside session times.
