A life goals self-assessment

Is your wheel whole?

Most of us are pouring everything into one or two areas of life while the rest quietly go flat. This tool walks you through ten areas of a whole life, has you name your biggest dreams for the areas that need it most, and tests those dreams before you chase them.

Start your assessment ↓

Four short steps. Your answers are saved automatically, only for you, right in this browser.

Step 1

Rate each area, honestly

For each area below, rate your current satisfaction and the level you'd like to reach a year from now. Then flag how much of a priority it is right now. There's no wrong answer — this is between you and yourself.

Step 2

Where the biggest gaps are

This ranks areas by the size of the gap between where you are and where you want to be, weighted by how much of a priority you marked them. Your top four move on to the next step.

Step 3

Write the dream, not just the number

For your top four focus areas, put your goals into words — as specific and as big as you actually want them. If it's a job, name the exact job. If it's a place, name the city. If it's a number, write the number. Don't edit yourself yet.

Step 4

Five honest questions

Before you chase a goal, it's worth testing it. These five questions are adapted from an old exercise for examining a dream before you commit to it — applied here to what you wrote in Step 3 for your top four focus areas.

A goal is worth the climb when it's actually yours, not someone else's expectation; when you're honest about the cost; when it doesn't come at another person's expense; and when it's not just about you. Sit with each question — there's no need for a polished answer.
