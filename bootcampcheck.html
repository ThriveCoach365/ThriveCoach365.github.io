This isn't a retreat you attend. It's a week of conversations that change how you decide.

Every camper gets a mentor and a small group of 6–8 for the week — not a lecture hall. The goal isn't a good week; it's better decisions for years afterward, about faith, family, relationships, money, and what comes next.

The week at a glance
  • Daily mentor small-groups, 6–8 campers each
  • A full afternoon of hands-on community service
  • Nightly worship and campfire fellowship
  • A distraction-free environment — devices are stored at check-in so conversations get full attention
  • A closing legacy project and commissioning ceremony

Questions parents ask first
Is my teen safe without a phone for five days?

Every mentor and volunteer is background-checked. A staff member is on the camp-office line at all times for real emergencies, and mentors are told to interrupt any session if a message comes through.

What if there's a family emergency during the week?

Devices are stored, not destroyed. Families get a direct camp-office number before drop-off, reachable at any hour.

My teen isn't big on church — will they feel out of place?

Thrive is built around Scripture, but the week leads with honest conversation, not performance. Doubt and questions are welcome.

What if we can't afford KES 15,000 right now?

Tell us on the form. A share of spots is set aside specifically for families who need it.
