What the program looks like

Structured, immersive, and built for growth.

Running from Sunday afternoon arrival (Nov 29) through Thursday lunch (Dec 3), each day weaves together spiritual grounding, emotional intelligence, physical discipline, and phone-free community.

Day 1 · Sun Nov 29
Arrival & Identity
Check-in, "Phone Hotel" drop-off, cabin team-building, Self-Awareness Snapshot, and evening session: Who Does God Say I Am?
Day 2 · Mon Nov 30
Mind & Emotions
Guarding the mind, Emotional Intelligence workshop, Laws of the Mind, self-awareness circles, and evening goal-setting seminar.
Day 3 · Tue Dec 1
Relationships & Service
Family dynamics, biblical friendships, God's design for dating & romance, plus an afternoon hands-on community service project.
Day 4 · Wed Dec 2
Digital Life & Stewardship
Challenge course, breaking free from addictions, scrolling with purpose, financial stewardship, 1-on-1 mentor check-ins, and open-mic Q&A.
Day 5 · Thu Dec 3
Purity & Commissioning
Sunrise prayer walk, legacy service task, Sexuality & Purity session, grace circle, commissioning ceremony, and closing lunch.

Core Curriculum Topics

  • Identity: Who does God say I am?
  • Friendships: Navigating healthy vs. unhealthy peers
  • Family: From conflict to connection at home
  • Emotional Intelligence: Understanding triggers, feelings & self-control
  • Social Media & The Mind: Guarding your thoughts and scrolling with purpose
  • Dating & Romance: Red flags, green flags, and dealing with rejection
  • Sexuality & Personal Safety: God's design, purity, and preventing sexual abuse
  • Life Skills & Stewardship: Time management, personal hygiene, etiquette, and money
  • Faith vs. Feeling: Building a daily walk with Christ that lasts

🗓️ Arrival, Check-Out & Meals

  • Check-In: Day 1 (Sunday, Nov 29, 2026) afternoon arrival from 2:00 PM.
  • Check-Out: Day 5 (Thursday, Dec 3, 2026) following closing lunch, from 1:45 PM.
  • Meals Included: 12 meals (Day 1 dinner through Day 5 lunch) plus snacks, scheduled at least five hours apart.
  • Dietary Needs: Dietary restrictions and allergies collected at registration are accommodated.
  • Accommodation: Residential cabins/dorms with mentor small groups of 6–8.

📵 "Phone Hotel" & Safety Protocol

  • Device-Free Week: Phones and personal gadgets are collected, logged, and stored at check-in ("Phone Hotel").
  • Re-Entry Debrief: Devices are returned at check-out on Day 5 after the closing lunch with a short re-entry conversation.
  • 24/7 Emergency Contact: Parents/guardians receive a direct camp-office emergency number prior to camp.
  • Safeguarding: Background checks, child-protection training for staff/volunteers, and first-aid/medical readiness.

🎒 Camper Packing Checklist

  • Bible, journal, and pen
  • Comfortable athletic and work clothing, and closed-toe shoes
  • Weather-appropriate outdoor gear (early December can bring short rains)
  • Toiletries and a reusable water bottle

🚫 Leave Off-Camp

  • Mobile phones, tablets, laptops, gaming devices, or smartwatches (if carried for travel, they must be surrendered at the Phone Hotel)
  • Expensive jewelry, large amounts of cash, or valuables
  • Energy drinks, vapes, alcohol, or non-prescribed substances
  • Revealing or immodest clothing

Questions parents ask first

Good questions.

How do I register?

Use the Jotform registration link on this page (form.jotform.com/262450461253047) to reserve a spot for the Teen Bootcamp (November 29 – December 3, 2026).

Is my teen safe without a phone for the week?

Every mentor and volunteer is background-checked. A staff member is on the camp-office line at all times for real emergencies, and mentors are told to interrupt any session if a message comes through.

What if there's a family emergency during the week?

Devices are stored, not destroyed. Families get a direct camp-office number before drop-off, reachable at any hour.

My teen isn't big on church — will they feel out of place?

Thrive is built around Scripture, but the week leads with honest conversation, not performance. Doubt and questions are welcome.

What if we can't afford the fee right now?

Tell us on the form. A share of spots is set aside specifically for those who need it.
