What the week looks like

Structured, immersive, and built for growth.

From Sunday afternoon (29 Nov) to Thursday lunch (3 Dec), every day weaves together Scripture, emotional intelligence, physical discipline, and real community.

Day 1 · Sun 29 Nov
Arrival & Identity
Check-in, cabin team-building, a Self-Awareness Snapshot, and the evening session Who Does God Say I Am?
Day 2 · Mon 30 Nov
Mind & Emotions
Guarding the mind, an Emotional Intelligence workshop, Laws of the Mind, self-awareness circles, and a goal-setting seminar.
Day 3 · Tue 1 Dec
Relationships & Service
Family, biblical friendships, God's design for dating and romance, and an afternoon community service project.
Day 4 · Wed 2 Dec
Digital Life & Stewardship
Challenge course, freedom from addictions, scrolling with purpose, money and stewardship, one-to-one mentor check-ins.
Day 5 · Thu 3 Dec
Purity & Commissioning
Sunrise prayer walk, legacy service task, the sexuality and purity session, grace circle, commissioning, and closing lunch.

Core curriculum

  • Identity: who does God say I am?
  • Friendships: healthy and unhealthy peers
  • Family: from conflict to connection at home
  • Emotional intelligence: triggers, feelings, and self-control
  • Social media and the mind: guarding your thoughts, scrolling with purpose
  • Dating and romance: red flags, green flags, and rejection
  • Sexuality and personal safety: God's design, purity, and preventing abuse
  • Life skills and stewardship: time, hygiene, etiquette, and money
  • Faith and feeling: a daily walk with Christ that lasts

Everything you need to know.

Where we'll be, how check-in works, how we keep every camper safe, and what to pack.

📍 The venue and your teen's safety

We are hosting at Joy Homestay, a secure residential home in Nkoroi, Kajiado, ideal for a focused small-group week. Every camper is closely supervised by mentors, phones are kept safe in our “Phone Hotel”, and families receive a camp-office number that is answered at any hour.

Reserve a spot

Arrival, check-out and meals

  • Venue: Joy Homestay, a residential home in Nkoroi, Kajiado.
  • Drop-off: we will confirm the drop-off point, any group transport, and the exact pin with registered families before camp.
  • Check-in: Day 1, Sunday 29 November 2026, from 2:00 PM.
  • Check-out: Day 5, Thursday 3 December 2026, after the closing lunch, from 1:45 PM.
  • Meals: 12 vegetarian meals, from Day 1 dinner to Day 5 lunch: a hearty breakfast, a medium lunch, and a light dinner, at least five hours apart.
  • Special diets: allergies and dietary needs shared at registration are catered for.
  • Rooms: separate boys' and girls' rooms, with mentor small groups of 6–8.

Phones and safety

  • Phone Hotel: phones and gadgets are collected and logged at check-in, so conversations get everyone's full attention.
  • Return: devices go back at check-out on Day 5, with a short chat about healthy phone use at home.
  • Emergencies: parents and guardians get a direct camp-office number before camp.
  • Safeguarding: every mentor and volunteer is background-checked, and a first-aid kit and medical plan are in place throughout.

What to pack

  • Bed linen: sheets, a blanket or duvet, and a pillowcase for four nights.
  • Bible, journal, and pen.
  • Modest, comfortable clothes for sport and work, plus a warm layer for cool mornings and evenings.
  • Closed shoes for walking, chores, and outdoor activities.
  • Rain gear: early December can bring short rains.
  • Toiletries, a bath towel, and a reusable water bottle.
  • Any prescribed medication, clearly labelled, to hand to a mentor at check-in.

Please leave at home

  • Phones, tablets, laptops, gaming devices, and smartwatches. If one comes for the journey, it goes straight into the Phone Hotel.
  • Jewellery, large amounts of cash, and other valuables.
  • Energy drinks, vapes, alcohol, and non-prescribed substances.
  • Outside snacks. Meals are provided at set times.

Refunds and transfers

We plan food, beds, and mentors around every registered camper, so here is how refunds and transfers work. We will always treat families with kindness and fairness.

  • If we cancel, or cannot offer a place: you receive a full refund, or a transfer to the next Thrive bootcamp, whichever you prefer. The same applies if we cannot safely meet your teen's medical needs.
  • Cancel by 11:59 PM on Thursday 26 November: full refund, less any M-Pesa transaction charge.
  • Cancel from 10:00 AM on Friday 27 November until check-in: 50% refund, or a full credit toward a future Thrive programme, valid for 12 months. By then food and lodging are already committed.
  • No-shows, leaving early, or cancelling after check-in: no refund. If there is a genuine medical or family emergency, talk to us and we will consider a credit.
  • Transfers: you may pass your place to another teen aged 14–18 at no cost until check-in. They complete the registration form, and the fee already paid stays as paid. If you cancel and register again after 25 November, the late fee applies.
  • Campership/Sponsorship places are for the named camper and cannot be transferred.
  • How to ask: WhatsApp +254 717 585709 or email teensmentor365@gmail.com with your teen's name and the M-Pesa transaction code. Refunds go back to the paying number within 7 working days.
Questions parents ask first

Good questions.

How do I register, and when do I pay?

Complete the registration form, then pay by M-Pesa. Registering and paying by 11:59 PM on Wednesday 25 November 2026 secures the early fee of KES 7,000. On Thursday 26 and Friday 27 November the fee is KES 10,000, and registration closes at 11:59 PM on Friday 27 November. A place is confirmed once we have both your form and your payment.

Is my teen safe at the venue, and without a phone?

Yes. The venue is a secure residential home, every mentor and volunteer is background-checked, and campers are supervised throughout. Phones are stored safely in the Phone Hotel, and the camp-office number is answered at any hour, so you can always reach us.

What if there's a family emergency during the week?

Devices are stored, never lost. You'll have a direct camp-office number before drop-off, and mentors will pause a session straight away if an urgent message comes through.

What about food, allergies, and medication?

Meals are vegetarian: a hearty breakfast, a medium lunch, and a light dinner. Please list any allergies, dietary needs, and medical details on the registration form. Prescribed medication should be clearly labelled and handed to a mentor at check-in.

Will you talk about sensitive topics like sexuality and addiction?

Yes, honestly and with care, always in a Scripture-grounded, age-appropriate way, led by trained mentors. These sessions include teaching on personal safety and how to speak up. If you would like to talk through the content before registering, WhatsApp us on +254 717 585709.

What does a typical day look like, and how are groups formed?

Days mix Scripture teaching, mentor small groups of 6–8, morning movement and practical work, rest, and evening worship and campfire time. Our leadership team arranges groups and rooms thoughtfully to support care and connection.

My teen isn't big on church. Will they feel out of place?

Thrive is built around Scripture, but the week leads with honest conversation, not performance. Doubts and questions are welcome here.

How do we get to the venue?

We will confirm the drop-off point, any group transport we arrange, and the exact pin in Nkoroi, Kajiado with registered families before camp. If you need help with transport, tell us on the form.

What is your refund and transfer policy?

In short: a full refund (less M-Pesa charges) if you cancel by 11:59 PM on Thursday 26 November, 50% or a future-programme credit from Friday 27 November until check-in, and free transfers to another teen aged 14–18. The full details are in the refunds and transfers section above.
