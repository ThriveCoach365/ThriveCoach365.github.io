Questions parents ask first

Good questions.

Why are there two bootcamps?

Feedback from parents and young people told us that high schoolers and campus-age adults need different things. The Teen Bootcamp (15–18) focuses on identity, family, friendships, and the pressures of school. The Young Adult Bootcamp (19–26) focuses on purpose, money, relationships, careers, and freedom from addictions.

Which bootcamp should I register for?

Still in high school: the Teen Bootcamp, December 6–10, 2026. On campus, out of school, or working: the Young Adult Bootcamp, January 4–8, 2027. If you are on the line, message us on +254 717 585709 and we will help you choose.

Is my teen safe without a phone for the week?

Every mentor and volunteer is background-checked. A staff member is on the camp-office line at all times for real emergencies, and mentors are told to interrupt any session if a message comes through.

What if there's a family emergency during the week?

Devices are stored, not destroyed. Families get a direct camp-office number before drop-off, reachable at any hour.

My teen isn't big on church — will they feel out of place?

Thrive is built around Scripture, but the week leads with honest conversation, not performance. Doubt and questions are welcome.

What if we can't afford the fee right now?

Tell us on the form. A share of spots is set aside specifically for those who need it.
